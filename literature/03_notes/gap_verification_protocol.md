# Gap Verification Protocol

Project: Tokyo-China Literature Workbench
Repo: E:\rsch\laborJapan
Status date: 2026-06-20

This protocol governs how the research gap for the Tokyo-China project is
verified before it may be asserted anywhere in the manuscript or outreach.

Provisional working title (NOT a confirmed gap):

  Chinese Residents and Opportunity-Risk Mismatch in Metropolitan Tokyo:
  A Public-Data Analysis of Housing, Accessibility, Services, and Disaster
  Exposure

Core rule:

  The final research gap must not be asserted, written into an introduction,
  or used in outreach materials until it is supported by a verified
  literature matrix and deep-reading notes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Gap Status Rule

- "Chinese Residents and Opportunity-Risk Mismatch" is a provisional working
  title only. It is not yet a confirmed gap.
- No final gap claim is allowed before systematic literature verification is
  complete.
- No introduction, abstract, outreach email, or grant-style statement should
  claim that the topic is novel until the evidence matrix supports that claim.
- Novelty must NOT be claimed around co-ethnic networks, spatial assimilation,
  or immigrant location choice in Tokyo. The Sun 2026 Cities paper already
  occupies that line (see Section on Sun 2026 below). The only candidate
  differentiation is the shift toward opportunity-risk mismatch and settlement
  infrastructure for Chinese residents, and that differentiation must itself be
  verified against real literature before it is asserted.

### Sun 2026 positioning (reason for caution)

The Sun 2026 Cities paper already covers a closely related Tokyo immigrant
settlement line:

- Tokyo metropolitan area
- 242 municipalities
- 2012-2018
- Chinese, Vietnamese, Filipino, and Brazilian immigrants
- co-ethnic networks
- spatial assimilation
- dynamic spatial Durbin model

Therefore the project must not claim novelty around co-ethnic networks, spatial
assimilation, or immigrant location choice in Tokyo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Required Evidence Base Before Gap Confirmation

The gap can be confirmed only after ALL of the following are satisfied:

- at least 80 verified literature records are entered into literature_matrix.csv
  or gap_verification_matrix.csv;
- at least 25 must-read items have deep-reading notes;
- all direct-overlap (A) and partial-overlap (B) items are reviewed;
- Sun 2026 is explicitly positioned;
- Japanese and English literature sources are both checked where possible;
- official data sources are separately documented and not confused with
  academic literature.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Search Sources

The search must include, where available:

- SciSpace
- Google Scholar

- CiNii
- J-STAGE
- publisher pages
- DOI records
- e-Stat, MOJ (Ministry of Justice), Tokyo Metropolitan Government, MLIT,

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Search Groups

The search must cover:

- Chinese migration to Japan
- Chinese residents in Tokyo
- Chinese immigrants and settlement in Japan
- foreign residents in Japan
- Tokyo immigrant settlement and residential segregation
- co-ethnic networks and spatial assimilation in Tokyo
- arrival infrastructure and migrant settlement infrastructure
- housing discrimination and housing constraints for foreigners in Tokyo/Japan
- service accessibility for immigrants, including healthcare, education,
  multilingual support, and everyday services
- disaster vulnerability, evacuation accessibility, and foreign residents in
  Tokyo/Japan
- opportunity structures, urban risk, and immigrant settlement
- spatial methods including LISA, spatial econometrics, 2SFCA, MGWR, typology,
  and spatial prediction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Gap Verification Matrix Logic

Every relevant item should be coded for:

- whether it covers Chinese residents;
- whether it compares Chinese and non-Chinese foreign residents;
- whether it covers housing;
- whether it covers transit accessibility;
- whether it covers service accessibility;
- whether it covers disaster risk;
- whether it covers arrival or settlement infrastructure;
- whether it covers opportunity-risk mismatch;
- whether it directly overlaps with the provisional topic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Overlap Categories

Use these categories:

- A_direct_overlap: the item may invalidate or substantially weaken the
  provisional gap.
- B_partial_overlap: the item overlaps with one module but does not invalidate
  the topic.
- C_theoretical_support: the item supports the framework.
- D_method_support: the item supports methods only.
- E_background_only: the item provides context only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Decision Rule

The provisional topic can be retained only if no A_direct_overlap item already
integrates all or most of the following in Metropolitan Tokyo:

- Chinese residents or Chinese nationals;
- non-Chinese foreign resident comparison;
- housing constraints;
- transit or accessibility;
- service accessibility;
- disaster or risk exposure;
- opportunity-risk typology or mismatch.

If such direct-overlap literature exists, the topic must be revised.
