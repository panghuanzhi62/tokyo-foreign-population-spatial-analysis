# Automated Discovery Next Steps

This file explains how the automated harvest output is used. The output lives
under outputs/literature_discovery/<run-id>/ and is PRELIMINARY.

## Order of work

1. Inspect A_direct_overlap_candidate first.
   These are the items most likely to threaten the provisional gap. Open
   direct_overlap_candidates.md and review every A candidate before anything
   else.

2. Manually verify DOI and publisher pages.
   For each A and B candidate, confirm the DOI resolves to the correct record
   and read the publisher landing page / abstract. Discard records whose DOI or
   source cannot be verified.

3. Use SciSpace for deep review of the high-risk candidates.
   Deep-read the verified A and B candidates in SciSpace to determine what they
   actually cover (Chinese residents, comparison groups, housing, transit,
   services, disaster, arrival infrastructure, opportunity-risk typology, and
   whether the study area is Metropolitan Tokyo).

4. Then update literature_matrix.csv and gap_verification_matrix.csv.
   Promote verified items into the matrices with full coding. Mark
   verified_source and must_read appropriately. Position each relative to
   Sun 2026.

5. Do not write the manuscript introduction before this review is complete.
   No introduction, abstract, outreach, or grant statement may claim novelty
   until the verified matrix and deep-reading notes support it.

## Reminder

Automated categories are triage hints, not evidence. The final research gap for
the Tokyo-China project must not be asserted until it is supported by a verified
literature matrix and deep-reading notes.
