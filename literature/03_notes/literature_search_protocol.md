# Literature Search Protocol

Project: Tokyo-China Literature Workbench
Repo: E:\rsch\laborJapan

## Purpose

The purpose of this protocol is to identify real literature before finalizing
the topic. The provisional working title is not a confirmed gap, and the
literature search exists to test it.

## Verification rules

- No unverified references should be used.
- Every cited item must have at least one of: DOI, stable URL, publisher page,
  official source, or an uploaded PDF stored in literature/01_pdfs/.
- SciSpace is used for discovery, Claude is used for extraction and matrix
  construction, and final selection is reviewed manually.

## Search sources

The search must include, where available:

- SciSpace
- Google Scholar
- Web of Science or Scopus if available
- CiNii
- J-STAGE
- publisher pages
- DOI records
- e-Stat, MOJ (Ministry of Justice), Tokyo Metropolitan Government, MLIT,
  GSI (Geospatial Information Authority of Japan), and other official sources
  for data context

## Search groups

The search must cover:

- Chinese migration to Japan
- Chinese residents Japan / Chinese immigrants Tokyo
- foreign residents Japan spatial assimilation
- Tokyo immigrant settlement and residential segregation
- co-ethnic networks Tokyo immigrant location choice
- arrival infrastructure / migrant settlement infrastructure
- housing discrimination foreigners Tokyo rental market
- service accessibility immigrants GIS healthcare education multilingual support
- disaster vulnerability foreign residents Tokyo evacuation accessibility
- opportunity-risk mismatch migrant settlement
- spatial methods: LISA, spatial econometrics, 2SFCA, MGWR, typology,
  spatial prediction
