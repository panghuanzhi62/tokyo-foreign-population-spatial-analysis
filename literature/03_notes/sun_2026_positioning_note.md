# Sun 2026 Positioning Note

Sun 2026 (Cities) is the key boundary-setting paper for this project.

## What Sun 2026 already covers

- Tokyo metropolitan area
- 242 municipalities
- 2012-2018
- Chinese, Vietnamese, Filipino, and Brazilian immigrants
- co-ethnic networks
- spatial assimilation
- dynamic spatial Durbin modeling

## Implications for the Tokyo-China project

- The Tokyo-China project should NOT replicate that design.
- The Tokyo-China project should EXTEND the literature toward:
  - opportunity-risk mismatch
  - service accessibility
  - housing constraints
  - disaster exposure
  - Chinese residents versus non-Chinese foreign residents
- All foreign residents can be retained as a background benchmark, but the
  core comparison should be Chinese residents versus non-Chinese foreign
  residents.
- The paper must avoid "first study" or "no study has examined" language until
  the gap verification matrix is complete.
