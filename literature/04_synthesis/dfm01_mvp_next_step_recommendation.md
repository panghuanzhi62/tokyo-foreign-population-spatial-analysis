## Recommended next step: A. Prepare a one-page visual brief

The DF_M01 MVP results are now stable and fully diagnosed: baseline X-M-Y models, complete-case
exclusion audit, collinearity robustness, alternative M scaling, timing robustness, residual
Moran's I diagnostics, and explicit spatial robustness (SLX, spatial-error, spatial-lag) all
point to the same consolidated conclusion - a robust positive population-to-service alignment
(X -> M), a robust short-term service-to-growth null (M -> Y), and no causal-mediation claim.
This is a defensible, self-contained finding suitable for a compact internal visual brief.

A one-page visual brief is the right next step because:
- the result set is internally consistent and has survived multiple robustness families, so a
  concise visual synthesis (a coefficient/robustness summary figure plus a short caption) can be
  produced without new modelling;
- it consolidates the existing committed tables and notes into a single shareable artifact for
  internal review, before any wider commitment.

## Why not the other options yet

- B. Draft full manuscript results section: premature. A results section draft already exists,
  but the broader paper needs theory framing, literature positioning, and likely M05/M06
  complementary sources before a full draft is warranted.
- C. Validate M05/M06 complementary M sources: this is a substantive expansion that should
  follow a decision to broaden M, not precede the brief.
- D. Extend rail/housing coverage to 251: useful robustness, but the 218-case estimand is
  already defensible and the conclusions are stable; full-coverage extension is a later
  refinement.
- E. Contact external scholars: not yet. The results are stable enough for a compact internal
  brief but not for broad outreach or paper submission; outreach should wait until the brief is
  reviewed and the scope (single-M MVP vs composite-M paper) is settled.

## One-line recommendation

Prepare a one-page visual brief consolidating the DF_M01 MVP results; defer outreach and full
manuscript drafting until the brief is reviewed. Do not claim causal mediation.
