# DF_M01 MVP - Spatial Residual Diagnostics (Synthesis / Manuscript-Facing Note)

Project: Chinese Residents and Opportunity-Risk Environments in Metropolitan Tokyo
Repo: E:\\rsch\\laborJapan   Branch: agentic-upgrade   Date: 2026-06-26
Cautious language. No causal-mediation claim. No novelty claim. Main substantive
interpretation (WEAK_PARTIAL_MECHANISM_EVIDENCE) unchanged.

## What was done

Queen-contiguity spatial weights (row-standardized) were built for the 218 complete-case
municipalities from the local N03-20250101 boundary, and residual Moran's I was computed for
the four growth-side target models (primary Model 2, primary Model 4, and their best
reduced-collinearity variants that drop population_density). Because the queen graph contains
one island (Katsuura-shi, 12205, whose peninsula neighbors are among the excluded peripheral
municipalities), a k-nearest-neighbor (k=6) sensitivity weighting was also used.

## Finding

All four models show statistically significant NEGATIVE residual spatial autocorrelation
under both queen (Moran's I about -0.09 to -0.10, permutation p about 0.015-0.020) and KNN-6
(Moran's I about -0.08, permutation p about 0.019-0.024) weights. The result is stable to the
weighting choice, so it is not an artifact of the single island.

Negative residual autocorrelation means adjacent municipalities tend to have DISSIMILAR
growth-model residuals - a spatial dispersion / local-competition pattern rather than
clustering.

## Interpretation (manuscript-facing)

SPATIAL_RESIDUALS_REQUIRE_SPATIAL_MODEL_ROBUSTNESS.

The core MVP conclusions are not overturned: the M -> Y null (DF_M01 service infrastructure
in 2024 is not associated with subsequent 2024-2025 Chinese-stock growth) is robust - a null
is not generated by negative residual autocorrelation, and it already held across collinearity
specifications, service scalings, and timing windows. Likewise the X -> M positive association
and the Model 3 commercial-density interaction are unaffected (those are service-side, not
growth-side, models). However, because the growth-side residuals show statistically
significant spatial structure, the OLS independence assumption is violated, and the manuscript
should report a spatial-error / spatial-lag (or SLX) robustness model for the growth-side
results before making any strong claim about the absence of a service-to-growth relationship.

The substantive reading is unchanged and cautious: the evidence is consistent with migrant
support-organization infrastructure FOLLOWING existing Chinese population (X -> M), not
DRIVING its subsequent growth (M -> Y null); these are temporally ordered associations, NOT
causal mediation.

## Recommended next step

Fit spatial-error and/or spatial-lag robustness versions of Models 2 and 4 on the 218
complete-case sample using the queen and KNN-6 weights, and report whether the growth-side
null and interactions persist under explicit spatial dependence.
