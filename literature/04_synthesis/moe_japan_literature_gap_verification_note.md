This note supports the Ministry of Education project application by summarizing the
Japanese-literature and Japanese-source grounding currently available, what is usable
now, what must be verified before formal citation, and the risk controls applied. It
draws only on records already documented and verified in this repository (Round 01/02
matrices, the official source registry, and the DF_M01..M04 service-infrastructure
profiles). It asserts no final research gap and no novelty.

---

## A. Current application strengths

- A clear, defensible positioning against the boundary paper Sun 2026 (Cities), which
  already covers Tokyo metropolitan immigrant location choice, co-ethnic networks, and
  spatial assimilation (242 municipalities, 2012-2018, dynamic spatial Durbin). The
  application's differentiation is the opportunity-risk environment plus a
  Chinese-vs-non-Chinese foreign-resident comparison.
- A verified, multi-module Japanese/Japan-focused evidence base across: Chinese
  residents in/near Tokyo (Zhang 2020; Wang & Fujii 2021; Takamatsu 2020; Abe
  1999/2000), housing constraints (Sugasawa & Harano 2023/2026; Suzuki et al. 2022; Kim
  2026), settlement environment (Lim et al. 2024), service/administrative background
  (Mori 1997; Nakato 2014; Saito ed. 2006), and disaster vulnerability of foreign
  residents in Tokyo (Adu-Gyamfi & Shaw 2021).
- A strong, reproducible official-data foundation already collected locally: ISA
  Statistics on foreign residents (Chinese stock panel), e-Stat Census 2020 and
  Economic Census 2021, NLNI N03 boundaries, N02 railway, L01 land price, and the
  DF_M01 registered-support-organization registry.
- A documented, transparent search-and-verification workflow that separates academic
  literature from official data and records stable URLs/DOIs (no PDFs committed).

## B. Main gaps in Japanese literature grounding

- The classic Japanese geography of Chinese settlement (e.g., Yamashita Kiyomi on
  Chinatowns/shinkakyo; Zhang Huijing on Ikebukuro; Chen Tianxi on identity/nationality)
  and the Chinese-domestic scholarship (Nie Dening; Ren Na & Chen Yande; Zhu Anxin) are
  named but NOT yet bibliographically verified in this repo.
- No verified record performs an explicit Chinese-vs-non-Chinese foreign-resident
  comparison (module 9), and none integrates the full opportunity-risk combination
  (housing + accessibility + services + disaster + typology) for Chinese residents in
  Tokyo (module 10). These are positioning opportunities, not a proven gap.
- Most Chinese-specific records are qualitative, small-N case studies; the housing and
  disaster evidence that is strongest is foreign-resident-general, not Chinese-specific.
- Academic (as opposed to official) sources for rail accessibility, danchi/public
  housing, suburban shrinkage, and service accessibility in Tokyo are thin in the
  current verified set.

## C. Sources that appear usable now (verified)

- Zhang 2020 (DOI 10.4157/grj.93.1) - Chinese white-collar housing choice, Tokyo metro.
- Adu-Gyamfi & Shaw 2021 (DOI 10.3390/ijerph18084061) - foreign-resident disaster
  vulnerability, Tokyo (closest spatial-risk comparator + method).
- Sugasawa & Harano 2023 (DOI 10.1016/j.jjie.2023.101273) - rental discrimination,
  Tokyo 23 wards (housing-constraint anchor).
- Suzuki et al. 2022 (DOI 10.1016/j.jhe.2022.101879) and Sugasawa & Harano 2026
  (DOI 10.1016/j.jhe.2026.102134) - housing-discrimination support.
- Lim et al. 2024 (DOI 10.1007/s10901-024-10160-3) - residential satisfaction,
  Toshima, Tokyo.
- Abe 1999 (DOI 10.4200/jjhg1948.51.23) and Abe 2000 (DOI 10.4157/grj1984a.73.9_694) -
  historical background (background only).
- Nakato 2014 (DOI 10.19024/jajls.17.1_36) and Mori 1997 (DOI 10.1057/9780230374522_8)
  - service-access background/comparators only.

## E. Official data sources already strong enough for application

- ISA Statistics on foreign residents (在留外国人統計) - Chinese stock/growth panel.
- e-Stat (Census 2020 00200521; Economic Census 2021 00200553) - denominators and
  commercial-density control (built 251/251 locally).
- NLNI N03-20250101 (boundaries), N02-22 (railway), L01-24 (land price) - spatial frame
  and accessibility/housing-cost variables, held locally.
- DF_M01 registered-support-organization registry (ISA/MOJ) - institutional
  migrant-support infrastructure by municipality (built locally).
These support data-source inventory, variable construction, and policy/service context;
they do NOT by themselves establish an academic research gap.

## F. Risk control

- No "fills a domestic/international blank" / "first study" language; use "relatively
  limited", "room for extension", "requires further systematic testing".
- Keep the Chinese-vs-non-Chinese comparison and integrated opportunity-risk framing as
  positioning, not as a confirmed gap.
- Separate academic literature from official data; never present official data as
  evidence of novelty.
- No applicant identity information and no commercial-database/career framing in the
  application (esp. Section-three/data-preparation materials).
- Official spatial/hazard layers marked "license and usability to be confirmed" before
  use; OTIT supervising-organization list flagged as needs_verification (a potential new
  M source requiring separate justification).
- No new Tokyo models run; no new M-source searching beyond what supports the Japanese
  materials section; no raw/GIS/local-only/correspondence files staged.
